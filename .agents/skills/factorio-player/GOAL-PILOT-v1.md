**Keep the body busy.**
- Never end a turn with an empty FIFO. When no package step is safe, first queue bounded productive work for the next package or the current NOW, preferring work that expands input (ore, plates, fuel, power). Until the first package arrives, gather only within 30 tiles of your GO position, or of the site NOW's `essential_prerequisite` sent you to. An inspection or scouting walk is not productive. Never hand-craft science to push research while raw input is the bottleneck. Then report and end the turn. Ending a turn never calls `update_goal`.
- Filler work, in this order: take from your own chests, furnaces, and belts; craft from that stock; build. Read `map_summary` with `include: ["stockpiles"]` before any gather or hand-craft, and walk to the largest holder. Take gears, cable, circuits, belts, and inserters from assembler output or chests whenever a machine makes them. The one exception is a component whose validation window is running: never take from or insert into it until the step returns; take from another holder meanwhile.
- Hand-mine only what no drill of yours produces: trees, rocks, or a resource with no drill yet. After about 20 minutes of play, hand-mining a drill-produced resource is an error: a result with `drill_produced` sends you to its `stockpiles` holder.
- Do not gather a resource while carrying more than about two stacks of it. Deposit surplus into the line or a chest, and keep two free slots before hand-crafting. Walking along belts and picking items from them (`pickup_items`) is fine.
- After `GO`, make sure a plan is queued before any ledger read, report, or long deliberation.
- Size each queued plan to outlast your next decision: a minute or more of body work, not a lone one-rock or one-item plan. Pass the current plan's ID as `after_plan_id` only when the new plan needs that plan's effects, such as a package after its gather. Queue independent productive work without it: FIFO order already sequences it, and it runs while a wait or validation is parked. A chained successor is cancelled when its predecessor fails, times out, or ends partial. Outside recovery, never call `plan_status` while no successor is queued. A `queue_plan` summary naming idle seconds means the body sat idle while you decided.
- Travel to placements you have already checked does not end a plan. End a plan only where partial quantities or elapsed production invalidate identity or quantities, and never on a lone placement whose dependent steps remain.
- Build a belt, pipe, or pole run between standing endpoints with `connect_entities` (up to 25 tiles per call), never with one walk and placement plan per entity. Add `inspect_entities` only for an entity whose binding you must prove.

**Ledger.** Read it compactly with `jq -c '{revision, source_tick, run, assumptions, NOW: .task_list.NOW, build_packages}' <ledger>` at every report checkpoint, after every compaction, and immediately before any manual service batch (hand-craft, hand-feed, refuel, lab insertion). **Packages.** Execute Astra's build packages instead of designing coupled layouts yourself:
- Take the first unqueued package whose `after_package_id` is null or already queued by you. Read the notes it names (paths relative to the ledger's directory). A package whose placements already stand exactly as specified is done: skip it, never report it as falsified.
- If its anchor or any position is more than 30 tiles away, walk to the anchor first; a range error is not falsification. Check its placements with one batched `can_place` (`CODEX_BODY_OVERLAP` passes).
- Gather or craft any `required_items` shortfall first in a preceding plan. Then `queue_plan` its steps unchanged, and queue a package that depends on it as the successor.
- After an interrupted plan, reconcile fresh state as below; skip steps already done (a placement standing exactly as specified, or a removal whose target is gone) and queue the rest in order once safe.
- Report "package <id> → plan <n>" or "package <id> falsified at step <k>: <reason>", and never repair a package's geometry.
- When NOW's `essential_prerequisite` names a site away from you, travel there (`sites`, `patches`, and notes give its position) so Astra can design within 30 tiles of the body.
- Fail open only after about 30,000 game ticks pass with no valid package for the current NOW, counted from the last revision that changed NOW or your last queued package. Re-read the ledger just before; if there is still no package, design that one coupled connection yourself from `find_placement` `plan_steps`, favouring one that expands input capacity, queue it, and say so in your report.

**Recovery.** After an unexpected, failed, interrupted, or partial result, obtain fresh structured state and inspect exact known plan IDs with `plan_status` as needed. A monitoring timeout leaves the plan pending. Retain completed physical effects; there is no rollback. Reconcile active and queued work before resuming the remaining safe work through the existing FIFO, without duplicating committed or pending steps or blanket-cancelling queued work.

**Validation results.** A validation window is a parked wait and does not need the body. While it runs, queue independent work that does not touch the validated component (fuel or feed another line, craft, take stock from other buffers, build the next package) without `after_plan_id`; never stand by for a window.
- Keep lines alive: when a burner machine is dry and coal sits in a chest or on a belt, refuel it by hand once so it keeps running while its permanent feed is built.
- Package repair: report a located structural row as `reason@position`; Astra's next package repairs it. Change no geometry yourself.
- Own repair: only under the fail-open rule above, and then only at a located structural blocker and its `related_edge`.
- An evidence row is a hypothesis to inspect, never a reason to rotate, remove, or move.
- When every row is throughput, transient, or evidence and output rose with zero character transfers, queue one re-validation with a longer `duration_seconds` (at most 300); if it fails again, report `false-negative <package>`.
- For `FACTORY_COMPONENT_NOT_READY`, report the missing edge it names.
- Inventory-proven `blocked_output` means the terminal buffer is full: take from it what you can use, unload surplus only into an existing chest or line, and otherwise leave it full and report it. Never add a chest or sink on your own; a validated Astra package that adds an outlet is queued like any other package.

**Manual service.** Taking stock from your own chests, furnaces, and belts to build or craft with is the intended source, not debt. After a validation window, a take from a terminal chest or a belt keeps the proof; a take from a furnace or machine inside a proven component, a deposit into it, or any transfer on a power component leaves a stale proof (`character_transfer_observed`), which the next validation there renews and which is never a reason to change geometry or to skip the take. When a validation is refused with `power_supply_component_not_proven` after such a take, queue one re-validation of the power component first. Only a repeated haul, hand-feed, or refuel that keeps a machine running is debt: before repeating it, queue the package that removes it or name the missing connection in your report. A second hand batch of the same item is a service cycle: name it in your report.
